## 2. Target Audience
- **Target User:** Complete beginners who have never run, and people returning to running after years off or injury.
- **Core Tensions:** Feel intimidated by traditional running clubs, nervous about being too slow, worried about holding people back, afraid of looking foolish or getting injured.
- **Jobs to Be Done (JTBD):**
  - *Functional:* Safely build cardio stamina to run 20 continuous minutes.
  - *Emotional:* Feel capable, energized, and proud of physical progress without suffering.
  - *Social:* Belong to an encouraging group where walking is welcomed and nobody is ever left behind.

## 3. Top 5 Fears & Objections
1. **"What if I'm the slowest and hold everyone back?"**  
   *Answer:* Designated back-of-the-pack walking/running sweepers; we regroup at every turn; zero runners left behind.
2. **"What if I need to stop and walk?"**  
   *Answer:* The method is built on run-walk intervals (walk breaks are scheduled and celebrated, not seen as failing).
3. **"Will people judge my body or fitness level?"**  
   *Answer:* Everyone in this cohort is starting from scratch. Regular sneakers and comfy clothes are all you need.
4. **"Is this going to hurt or cause injury?"**  
   *Answer:* Slow, gradual progression designed to build joint resilience; coaches teach breathing and form before distance.
5. **"Is this an intense club for marathoners?"**  
   *Answer:* No. This is exclusively a beginner cohort. Elite runners have separate groups; this space is 100% beginner-focused.

## 4. One-Sentence Promise
"Go from your couch to running 20 minutes comfortably and with confidence—at your own pace, with a friendly group that never leaves anyone behind—in 30 days."
