Worried you are too slow? Read this first.

Most people quit running because they sprint out the door on day one, burn out their lungs in two minutes, and feel terrible. Here is how we do it:

Walking is built into the plan.

When the watch says walk, everyone walks. That is how you build stamina without feeling awful. Walk breaks are scheduled and celebrated.

Nobody gets left behind.

A coach stays at the very back of the pack the whole time. You will never be running on your own or holding anyone up.

Wear what you have.

You do not need expensive running gear or a certain body type. Normal trainers and comfortable clothes are plenty.
