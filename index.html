Worried you are too slow? Read this first.

Most people quit running because they sprint out the door on day one, burn out their lungs in two minutes, and feel terrible. Here is how we do it:

Walking is built into the plan.

When the watch says walk, everyone walks. That is how you build stamina without feeling awful. Walk breaks are scheduled and celebrated.

Nobody gets left behind.

Coach Jon Dow stays at the very back of the pack the whole time. You will never be running on your own or holding anyone up.

Wear what you have.

You do not need expensive running gear or a certain body type. Normal trainers and comfortable clothes are plenty.

What happens at session one

Here is the exact schedule for your first 45 minutes so you know what to expect:

00–10 min

Say hello

Meet Coach Jon Dow and grab your name tag at Central Park Inner Circle, Connaught Place (near Gate 2). A relaxed, friendly introduction.

10–20 min

Easy warm-up

We walk together on the paved park path and do a few simple leg stretches. No jumping jacks or burpees, just getting your joints loose.

20–35 min

The run-walk

We jog gently for 60 seconds, then walk for 90 seconds. You set whatever speed feels comfortable.

35–45 min

Cool down

A quick stretch under the park trees, water, and time to ask any questions before heading home.

The 30-day plan

Four simple weeks to get your legs and lungs used to running:

Week 1

Getting started

1 minute of easy jogging, 2 minutes of walking. The only goal is showing up and learning how your body feels.

Week 2

Finding your pace

We teach you how to jog at a talking pace so you do not run out of breath. The intervals gently lengthen.

Week 3

Stretching the distance

Your running intervals get a little longer, but your body is ready for it now. Your breathing feels steady.

Week 4

The 20-minute run

You jog 20 minutes steadily alongside your group. No stopping, no gasping for air. You cross the 30-day mark.

Common questions

Honest answers to the most common beginner worries:

I get out of breath walking up stairs. Can I actually do this?
Yes. We start with 60 seconds of gentle jogging, followed by plenty of walking. You do not need to be in shape before you come. Getting in shape is why we are here.
What if I am the slowest person in the group?
Coach Jon Dow stays right at the back the entire time, and we regroup at every turn. You will never hold anyone up.
Do I need special running shoes?
No. A standard pair of clean trainers and everyday sportswear will do fine for the first month.
What if I miss a session?
Don't worry about it. We post the workout in our group chat so you can do it on your own time if you want to.
Where and when do we meet?
We meet at Central Park Inner Circle, Connaught Place (CP) every Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday at 6:00 AM.

Ready for your first run?

You do not need to be fast, and you do not need to run the whole time. You just need to show up in your trainers. We will take care of the rest together.
